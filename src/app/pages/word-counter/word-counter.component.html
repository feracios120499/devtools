About Word Counter

This Word Counter tool provides comprehensive text analysis including word count, character count, and word frequency distribution. Perfect for writers, content creators, and anyone who needs to analyze text content.

Features:

  • Real-time word counting: Get instant word and character counts as you type
  • Word frequency analysis: See how often each word appears in your text
  • Interactive highlighting: Hover over words in the distribution to highlight them in the editor
  • Advanced text editor: Monaco editor with syntax highlighting and professional editing features
  • Copy and paste support: Easy text manipulation with clipboard integration
  • Sample text loading: Load example text to test the functionality
  • Privacy focused: All processing happens locally in your browser - no server upload

Use cases: Content writing, blog post analysis, academic paper review, social media content optimization, SEO content analysis, and text editing workflows.

All text analysis happens in your browser - your content is never sent to any server.
