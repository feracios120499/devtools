What is a Word Counter?

A Word Counter is an online text analysis utility that reads any piece of writing and reports the total number of words, characters, and the frequency of every unique term. It turns a raw block of text into a structured view of how your content is actually composed, which is essential for writers, students, SEO specialists, and developers working with textual data.

Why Use a Word Counter?

Most writing tasks come with explicit length constraints - essays have minimum word counts, news snippets and tweets enforce hard character limits, and SEO guidelines recommend target ranges for headings, meta descriptions and body content. A Word Counter gives you an immediate, objective number so you can plan, trim or expand your text with confidence instead of guessing.

It also exposes over-used terms through the word frequency list, making it easy to spot repetition, balance keyword density, and tighten your writing style.

How Text Statistics Are Computed

Word and frequency counting follow a deterministic pipeline designed for typical prose:

  • Normalization: the input is converted to lower case so that "Tool" and "tool" are treated as the same term.
  • Tokenization: punctuation and every non-word character are replaced with whitespace, then the string is split on runs of whitespace.
  • Filtering: empty tokens produced by consecutive separators are discarded, leaving only real words.
  • Character counting: the character total uses the raw input length, so spaces, punctuation, and line breaks are all included.
  • Sentence & paragraph boundaries: paragraphs are visible as blank lines in the editor while sentence boundaries follow standard punctuation marks (., !, ?), which you can verify visually in your own text.

FAQ

Is this Word Counter free to use?

Yes, it's completely free with no registration, ads, or usage limits.

Do you store my text?

No, all processing happens locally in your browser. Your text never touches our servers.

How are words counted?

The tool lowercases your text, replaces punctuation with spaces, splits on whitespace and counts each non-empty token as a word.

Can I analyze large documents?

Yes, the Monaco-based editor handles long texts efficiently, limited only by your browser's memory.

What is the word distribution list?

It is a frequency table that shows every unique word and how many times it appears in your text, sorted from the most to the least frequent.

Can I use this tool for SEO keyword density?

Yes. The word frequency distribution works as a simple keyword density indicator, helping you see which terms dominate your content.

Does the Word Counter work offline?

Yes. After the page loads, all counting and analysis runs in your browser, so it continues to work without an active internet connection.
