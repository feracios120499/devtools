What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is a scrambled, Latin-looking placeholder text derived from Cicero's classical treatise De finibus bonorum et malorum (written in 45 BC). Since the 1500s, typesetters have used it as dummy text to fill layouts without distracting readers with meaningful content.

Modern Lorem Ipsum keeps the familiar letter distribution and natural-looking word lengths of Latin, so paragraphs, sentences, and words render realistically while typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy are still being designed.

Why Use Placeholder Text?

Designers use placeholder text to focus on layout, typography, and information hierarchy before final copy is ready. Real content often causes stakeholders to review wording instead of visual structure, so neutral filler text keeps the conversation on design.

Placeholder text also helps engineers stress-test components: long strings reveal line-height and overflow issues, while varying paragraph counts expose spacing and responsive breakpoint bugs early.

How Lorem Ipsum Is Generated

The tool draws from a curated seed corpus of classical Lorem Ipsum vocabulary. Words are picked with random selection so every run produces a fresh passage while keeping the familiar Latin look.

Basic grammar constraints shape the output: sentences contain between 8 and 22 words, paragraphs combine 3 to 7 sentences, each sentence starts with a capitalized word and ends with a period, keeping the rhythm readable.
